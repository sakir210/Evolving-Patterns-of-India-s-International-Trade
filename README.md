# Evolving-Patterns-of-India-s-International-Trade
The Graphs are uploaded in the Graph folder
This Project Relates to how import and export trends of India have evolved since 1990 to 2022 and how India’s exports and imports have changed over time and what it means for the economy. It shows that India’s trade has grown a lot since the 1990s due to globalization and policy reforms. However, the country has mostly faced a trade deficit because it imports more than it exports, especially oil. The study highlights that non-oil exports like services and manufacturing are growing strongly, which is a positive sign. In recent years, exports have increased sharply, helping to reduce the trade gap and even create a surplus. The project also explains how policies and global factors influence trade patterns. A key point is that India’s challenge is not weak exports, but high dependence on imports, especially energy. The work aims to show that while India is becoming stronger in global trade, it still needs to reduce import dependency. Overall, the motive of this study is to understand trade trends and suggest how India can move towards a more balanced and stable trade system.
